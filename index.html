About me

I like figuring things out. Especially when the data is messy, a process is still being done manually, or a report takes far longer to prepare than it should.

I’m a Data Analyst and Power BI Developer with experience across finance, operations, healthcare, logistics, and e-commerce. Most of my work sits somewhere between business intelligence, data analysis, automation, and building practical systems that make everyday work easier.

These days I work mainly with Power BI, DAX, Power Query, SQL, Excel, Looker Studio, Microsoft Fabric, Google Apps Script, Power Automate, and APIs. I enjoy taking a process from the “this is how we’ve always done it” stage and turning it into something more automated, reliable, and useful.

I’ve worked directly with international clients in fully remote environments, owning projects from understanding the requirement all the way through delivery. Whether it’s an executive dashboard, an ETL pipeline, a payroll automation system, or a complete operations platform, I care about building something people can actually use not just something that looks good in a demo.

I’m naturally curious, practical in how I approach problems, and always trying to get a little better at both the technical side of my work and the way I collaborate with people.
